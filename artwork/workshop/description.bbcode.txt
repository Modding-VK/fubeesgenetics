[h1]Bee Abusive Industry[/h1]
An industrial bee-processing addon for [b]Frackin' Universe[/b] on [b]OpenStarbound[/b].

This mod grew from my difficulties automating beekeeping. In the age of AI, its name plays on “Bee A.I.” and “Abusive Industry” — and AI Chips are part of the equipment recipes.

Want to know how everything works? Check out the Workshop discussion:

[url=https://steamcommunity.com/workshop/filedetails/discussion/3822748368/573805072991229995/]Learn more about the mod's mechanics[/url]

By Goblin Man.

[h2]Requirements[/h2]
Requires OpenStarbound and Frackin' Universe. The vanilla Starbound executable is not supported.

Haven't heard of OpenStarbound? Give it a look — it's worth it! :)

[h2]Genetic Queen Filter[/h2]
Sort inspected Queens and Young Queens using six genetic traits. Choose Equal or higher, Equal or lower, Indifferent, Equal, or Within range for each trait.

The interface keeps the reference profile visible, and the Approved and Rejected outputs support FU item networks and ITD.

[h2]Auto Refugee[/h2]
Automate bee trading using the rewards from the FU Bee Refuge. Sixteen shared slots accept Queens, Young Queens, and batches of five drones of the same species.

Hybrid bees cannot be traded here.

[h2]Genetic Hybridizer[/h2]
Breed a Queen and a Drone from different species to create new hybrid races. With 35 base species, the system allows up to 595 unique hybrid combinations.

Each hybrid combines traits from its parents and can produce resources associated with both species. Research Discs can improve the chance of a successful experiment.

[h2]Overload Apiaries[/h2]
Put your bees to work harder and increase resource and drone production — by making them suffer, of course.

The mod includes four models:

[list]
[*]Overload Apiary: a compact option for your colonies.
[*]Overload Large Apiary: more room for a larger operation.
[*]Ov. Framekeeper: boosts production while protecting the inserted frame from wear.
[*]Ov. Tri-Frame: supports three frames for more frame bonuses.
[/list]

Powered Overload Apiaries produce 20% more resources and drones. They keep working at normal speed without power, but the Queen's lifespan remains reduced to 80% of normal.

[h2]Bee Genetic Microscope[/h2]
Instantly reveal a bee's genetic data without producing Research, Pixels, or other rewards. It also supports item networks for automation.

[h2]Genetic Stabilizer[/h2]
Reduce a bee's mutation chance by half with each treatment. There's a catch: every treatment has a 40% chance of destroying the bee.
