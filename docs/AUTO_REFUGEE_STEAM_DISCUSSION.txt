[h1]Auto Refugee[/h1]

This guide explains how the Auto Refugee accepts bees and automatically exchanges them for the rewards defined by the Frackin' Universe Bee Refuge.

[h2]1. What goes into the machine[/h2]

[list]
[*]A Queen or Young Queen from Frackin' Universe
[*]Drones from Frackin' Universe
[*]Items from any other category are ignored and remain in storage
[/list]

The machine reads the bee's species and caste from its item parameters. It does not exchange an item merely because its name contains the word bee.

[h2]2. Queen and Young Queen exchanges[/h2]

Attention: Hybrid bees being untradeable is intentional!

A valid Queen is exchanged according to the matching Bee Refuge recipe for its species. A Young Queen uses the same species reward as the corresponding adult Queen.

[table]
[tr][th]Input[/th][th]Result[/th][/tr]
[tr][td]Queen[/td][td]The species-specific Bee Refuge resource[/td][/tr]
[tr][td]Young Queen[/td][td]The same species-specific resource as its adult Queen[/td][/tr]
[tr][td]Unrecognized or invalid bee[/td][td]No exchange; the item remains available[/td][/tr]
[/table]

The machine uses the live FU recipe data rather than a second reward table. This keeps the exchange aligned with the installed version of Frackin' Universe.

[h2]3. Drone exchanges[/h2]

Five Drones of the same species are exchanged together for [b]7 Pixels[/b]. The drones must form one matching group.

[list]
[*]Four drones are not enough.
[*]Drones from different species do not form a group.
[*]Genetic values may differ; the species must match.
[*]An incomplete group remains in storage and is not consumed.
[/list]

[b]Example[/b]

[list]
[*]Five drones of one species: one exchange for 7 Pixels.
[*]Three drones of one species and two of another: no exchange.
[*]Ten drones of one species: two exchanges, provided output space is available.
[/list]

[h2]4. Output and safety[/h2]

The Auto Refugee never consumes a bee unless the expected output can be delivered. If output storage is full or the reward cannot be resolved, the input is preserved for a later attempt.

Rewards remain in the machine until they are taken or transferred through an item network. A sale can finish delivering an already-confirmed reward when the machine is switched off; new sales do not begin while it is disabled.

[h2]5. Slots and item placement[/h2]

The machine has 16 shared storage slots in the native container interface.

[table]
[tr][th]Storage[/th][th]Use[/th][/tr]
[tr][td]Slots 0-15[/td][td]Bee inputs and pending/output items[/td][/tr]
[tr][td]Non-bee items[/td][td]Ignored by the exchange logic and kept in storage[/td][/tr]
[/table]

[h2]7. Nodes and item networks[/h2]

The machine follows the Terramart Shipments 3.5 node arrangement:

[table]
[tr][th]Node[/th][th]Function[/th][/tr]
[tr][td]Upper input node[/td][td]On/Off switch[/td][/tr]
[tr][td]Lower input node[/td][td]Item Network Input[/td][/tr]
[tr][td]Output node[/td][td]Item Network Output[/td][/tr]
[/table]

The upper switch is automatic when it is unwired. A connected low signal pauses new exchanges; a connected high signal allows sales. Disconnecting the switch restores automatic operation.

[h2]8. Improved Containers[/h2]

Improved Containers 2.11 is optional. When installed, the Auto Refugee keeps the native 16-slot window and supports the mod's sorting, search, and quick-stack actions.

The machine waits for storage-management acknowledgements before converting items. This protects bee parameters and prevents a sort or quick-stack action from being mistaken for a sale.

Breaking and replacing the machine preserves ordinary contents and any confirmed pending reward. Other Improved Containers machines keep their normal behavior.

[h2]9. Crafting and unlock[/h2]

The Auto Refugee is crafted at the Bee Station.
