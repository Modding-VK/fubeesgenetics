[h1]Overload Apiaries[/h1]

The Overload Apiaries force bees to work beyond what their bodies can safely endure.

They are designed for higher throughput, with a permanent cost to queen lifespan.

[h2]Power behavior[/h2]

Power is used to provide the speed bonus.

[list]
[*]With 12 W available, resource and drone production are increased by 20%.
[*]If power is interrupted, the apiary keeps operating at normal speed.
[*]A power failure does not stop production.
[*]A power failure does not restore the queen's lost lifespan.
[*]The machine's belt LEDs indicate whether the power bonus is currently active.
[/list]

The power system only controls the bonus. It is not required for the apiary to continue working.

[h2]Queen lifespan[/h2]

The Overload Apiary accelerates queen aging as part of its core mechanic.

The queen has [b]70% of the normal lifespan[/b], whether the machine is powered or unpowered.

This penalty is intentional: the machine trades queen longevity for production throughput.

[h2]Production[/h2]

The apiary uses the normal Frackin Universe production system and bee genetics.

[list]
[*]The apiary defines the resource tables and base production rules.
[*]The bee provides its species, subspecies, genes, and production data.
[*]Powered operation applies the 20% production bonus.
[*]Unpowered operation uses normal production speed.
[*]Hybrid bees use their inherited lineage and predominant subspecies when production is calculated.
[/list]

The Overload Apiary does not randomly create capturable bee entities in the world. Bees remain managed by the apiary instead of being spawned as collectible entities. Captives till death!

[h2]Small Overload Apiary[/h2]

The small model is the compact version for standard colonies. It has 12 output slots arranged in 3 rows of 4, plus one queen slot, one drone line, and one frame line.

It requires 12 W for the +20% powered bonus.

[h2]Overload Large Apiary[/h2]

The Large model uses the larger Frackin Universe apiary layout for bigger colonies. It has 20 output slots arranged in 5 rows of 4, plus two drone lines and two frame lines.

It also requires 12 W and applies the same +20% powered bonus. Its larger capacity does not change the queen lifespan penalty.

[h2]Summary[/h2]

[list]
